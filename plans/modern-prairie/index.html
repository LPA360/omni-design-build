The plan

The two-story option

This version of the Modern Prairie Residence keeps the same 1,800 square feet of living space and stacks it on two floors. The main floor is open from the kitchen through the dining area to a great room with a 20-foot ceiling. The primary suite stays downstairs, with its own bath and walk-in closet.

Upstairs you get two more bedrooms, a full bath and a loft that looks down into the great room. A covered balcony off the second floor sits above the covered patio, so there is outdoor living on both levels.

The film above walks the single-story layout. Everything described below is the two-story option.

Main level

First floor

You come in through a covered porch to an 8 by 8 foyer. From there the house opens up: the kitchen and its island, the dining area beyond it, and a great room that runs 26 feet deep under a 20-foot ceiling. The walk-in pantry sits right off the kitchen, and the covered patio is at the back of the house next to the dining area.

The primary suite takes the other side of the main floor. The bedroom is 14 by 18, the bath has a soaking tub and a separate shower, and the walk-in closet is 10 by 8. The laundry room is a few steps from the suite.

Upper level

Second floor

The stairs land at an 18 by 12 loft that overlooks the great room. There is a bedroom on each side of the loft, each with its own closet, and a full bath between them.

The covered balcony is 18 by 10 and gives the upstairs its own place to sit outside. The rest of the second floor is left open to the great room below, which is where the 20-foot ceiling comes from.
